Futuristic AI Robot

Campaign Design Product Concept UI Design AI Prompting

OVERVIEW:

Set in a speculative future world, this project explores how a promotional marketing campaign introduces and sells an everyday Family AI Robot to the public through a consistent multi-channel experience.

CONCEPT:

What if family time becomes a luxury? In a future shaped by social isolation and changing family structures, emotional AI emerges as an everyday form of companionship and care.

DURATION:

6 Weeks

TOOLS:

Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Generative AI

ROLE:

Research, Visual & UI Design

01. Overview

Futuristic AI Robot - Speculative product design

This project explores how emerging shifts in ageing, care and emotional AI could shape future products. Through faint signals and a what-if scenario, the Family AI Robot was developed as a speculative artefact and introduced through a multi-channel promotional campaign.

02. Background

What if family time becomes a luxury?

In the future, people work longer hours and live farther apart. Elderly parents spend their days alone, while children grow up surrounded by screens instead of care. Emotional AI has evolved beyond assistants — it learns, adapts, and connects.

"How might we design a household AI companion that responds to the needs of an ageing population while providing both practical and emotional support?"

03. Faint Signals

Signal 1 — The rise of aging societies

Global population ageing is increasing demand for elderly care, driving governments and industries to explore household AI robots as part of future care systems.

Signal 2 — Emotional AI as the new caregiver

AI is moving beyond functional assistance toward companionship and emotional support, suggesting a future role for robots in addressing caregiver shortages and social isolation.

04. What-if Scenario

In 2075, the government launches the Family AI Robot Registration Scheme to address an ageing population and growing demand for home-based care. Certified AI companions support daily routines, health monitoring and emotional wellbeing.

As these robots become part of everyday life, concerns emerge around emotional dependency, delayed responses and the growing role of AI in family care.

AI companionship shifts from a consumer product to a regulated part of everyday society.

05. Selling Points

Comprehensive and Reliable Elderly Care
Emotional and Personalized Companionship
Compliance and Security Assurance
Customizable Solutions
Social and Family Value

06. Promotion Strategy

Touch point 1

Urban Billboards

High-impact urban advertising focusing on emotional care and domestic utility to immediately capture attention and normalize the product in everyday public life.

Touch point 2

Brand website

A clean product interface explaining features, customization, and specs to guide potential buyers from curiosity to purchase decision.

Touch point 3

Editorial Spread

Visual storytelling and print grid layouts designed to address public trust, safety, and brand value for high-consideration buyers.
